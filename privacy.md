# Privacy Policy – Shorts Clipper
Last updated: 24 November 2026

Shorts Clipper is a personal tool used only by its owner to upload
short video clips to the owner's own YouTube channel.

- It uses the YouTube Data API with the `youtube.upload` permission only,
  to upload videos to the owner's channel.
- It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data
  of any other user.
- Google sign-in credentials are stored privately by the owner and are
  never shared with third parties.
- Use of YouTube data follows the YouTube Terms of Service
  (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy
  (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
